Overview

Heavy Transport Solutions for Defense and Industry

XTT (Xtreme Transport) delivers specialized heavy transport, logistics, and carrier solutions for defense contractors, industrial operators, and government agencies. Where standard carriers fail — in terrain, weight class, or mission sensitivity — XTT performs. Our team is built around the singular discipline of moving what others cannot.

XTT operates at the intersection of defense logistics and industrial transport, providing custom carrier configurations, armored transport support, and oversize/overweight load movement for sectors that cannot afford failure. Every XTT operation is planned and executed to the exacting standards of defense-grade logistics.

Products & Solutions

What We Offer

Heavy Equipment Transport

End-to-end heavy equipment transport solutions for construction, industrial, and government clients — from planning and permitting through final placement, including oversized and overweight load management.

Armored Vehicle Logistics

Specialized transport and staging of armored vehicles, tactical equipment, and sensitive defense assets — with full chain-of-custody documentation and security protocols meeting DoD requirements.

Oversize Load Solutions

Complete oversize and overweight load movement services including route surveys, escort coordination, permit acquisition, and specialized carrier configurations for loads that exceed standard highway limits.

Defense Contractor Carrier Services

Dedicated carrier services for prime defense contractors moving components, systems, and finished assets between manufacturing, testing, and delivery destinations under mission-sensitive conditions.

Industrial Equipment Movement

Heavy industrial equipment relocation, rigging, and transport for plant expansions, manufacturing consolidations, and capital equipment installations requiring precision placement and damage-free delivery.

Specialized Mission Transport

Custom-configured transport operations for unique mission requirements — including time-critical movements, restricted-access sites, and multi-modal transport chains requiring seamless coordination across carriers.

Target Markets

Who We Serve

◆

Department of Defense

Direct support to DoD installations, commands, and logistics operations requiring heavy transport of tactical, combat, and support equipment across domestic and staging environments.

◆

Prime Defense Contractors

Tier 1 and Tier 2 defense contractors transporting manufactured systems, subassemblies, and finished platforms between production sites, government acceptance facilities, and end-user delivery points.

◆

Heavy Industrial Operators

Manufacturing plants, processing facilities, and industrial operators moving large capital equipment, pressure vessels, turbines, and specialty components requiring oversized transport expertise.

Oil & Gas Field Operations
