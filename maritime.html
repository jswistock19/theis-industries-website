Case Study

Seal Healing — Theis Tekton Used to Coat Fuel Tanks

Maritime vessels operating in hostile environments faced a critical vulnerability: ammunition breaching fuel tanks, rendering vessels unable to operate, communicate, or reach their destination. Theis Tekton was deployed to solve an unsolvable problem.

Self-Sealing Fuel Tank Coating

From Vulnerability to Invulnerability

The Challenge: Maritime vessels under attack — ammunition would breach fuel tanks, causing catastrophic fuel loss. Without fuel, the vessel couldn't operate, communicate, or arrive at its destination.

The Solution: Theis Tekton was applied to coat the fuel tanks. The system performed so successfully that fuel tanks were regularly incorporated into the maintenance process as a standard protective measure.

The Result: During subsequent operations, ammunition breached the coated tanks — but the tanks immediately self-healed. Fuel was contained. The system remained fully operational. Communication continued. The vessel reached its destination.

Outcome

Tanks breached — and immediately healed. Fuel contained and available for use. Communication continued. Vessel operational. No mission compromise. A coating system that delivered when it mattered most.

Case Study

Port of Los Angeles — Spill Containment & Rail Integration

One of America's busiest ports faced a compounding problem: contaminated containment infrastructure, failing adhesion to drainage systems, and no commercially available solution. Theis Industries stepped in and solved it.

The Problem

Rail lines at the Port of Long Beach use oil spill pans to protect soil and water tables from petroleum contamination. Relatively new fiberglass pans became so saturated with petroleum they could no longer function as effective containment.

When new drain installations went in, contractors discovered they couldn't secure the pan to the drainage system — standard adhesives and sealants refused to bond to the oil-saturated fiberglass surface.

The contamination problem was compounding — pans failing, seals failing, no available solution on the market.

The Theis Solution

Theis Industries developed a specialized formulation to patch damaged pans and secure pipe to pan — ensuring proper adhesion despite complete oil saturation of the substrate.

No other product on the market could achieve this. The Tekton-based formulation bonded effectively to the contaminated fiberglass surface, restoring the spill containment functionality of the pans and providing a leak-free seal at pipe connections.

Result

Port of LA / Long Beach now uses Theis Industries for many projects across the port complex. A trusted, proven relationship built on solving what others couldn't.
