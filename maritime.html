Overview

Marine Technologies Proven at the Deepest Depths

Theis Maritime harnesses two decades of Theis Tekton™ performance in deep-sea, naval, and commercial marine environments. Our hull protection coatings, rapid underwater repair compounds, and corrosion systems are proven where no other product could survive — from the ocean floor to open ocean operating environments.

The cornerstone of Theis Maritime is our rapid-cure subsea repair polymer — a formulation that cures in 3 to 10 seconds underwater at depth, enabling emergency hull and structural repairs without dry-docking. No other product in the market matches this capability. For navies, commercial fleets, and offshore operators, this changes the economics and operational tempo of marine maintenance entirely.

Products & Solutions

What We Offer

Hull Protection Coating Systems

Tekton-based hull protection coatings providing long-life corrosion resistance, antifouling performance, and impact protection for naval vessels, commercial ships, and offshore structures operating in aggressive marine environments.

Rapid-Cure Underwater Repair Compound

Our flagship marine product — a polymer compound that cures in 3 to 10 seconds when applied underwater at depth. Enables emergency hull, structural, and pipeline repairs without dry-docking or surface recovery, saving time and operational cost.

Anti-Corrosion Marine Coatings

Full suite of marine-grade anti-corrosion coatings for topside, waterline, and below-waterline applications — providing barrier protection against salt, biological attack, and electrochemical corrosion on steel and aluminum marine structures.

Subsea Pipeline Protection

Coating and repair systems for subsea pipeline infrastructure — protecting against external corrosion, abrasion, and marine biological fouling in deepwater, shallow water, and tidal zone operating environments.

ROV-Applied Repair Systems

Tekton repair formulations packaged for remotely operated vehicle application — enabling subsea structural and coating repairs without diver entry, reducing risk and expanding operational depth capability.

Naval Platform Specialty Coatings

Defense-grade specialty coatings for naval vessels including radar-absorbent formulations, thermal barriers, non-skid deck systems, and corrosion protection for weapons systems housings and sensitive naval equipment.
