Overview

Advanced Materials for Energy Infrastructure

Theis Energy applies Tekton-based coatings and petroleum engineering expertise to protect critical oil, gas, power, and energy infrastructure from corrosion, pressure failure, and environmental degradation. Our pipeline coating systems, tank protection products, and power plant engineering extend asset life and reduce catastrophic failure risk.

From patented underground storage tank systems to anti-corrosion pipeline coatings proven in offshore environments, Theis Energy delivers the materials science that keeps America's energy infrastructure running. Our solutions protect the assets that cannot fail — eliminating the environmental liability and economic loss that corrosion-driven failures create.

Products & Solutions

What We Offer

Pipeline Anti-Corrosion Coating Systems

Long-life Tekton-based coating systems for oil, gas, and water pipelines — providing chemical, abrasion, and microbial corrosion resistance far exceeding conventional epoxy and polyurethane pipeline coatings.

Underground Storage Tank Systems (Patented)

Patented form-coded underground storage tank fill port and lining systems — preventing cross-contamination, extending structural life, and eliminating the environmental liability of corroded underground fuel storage assets.

Power Plant Protective Coatings

High-temperature, chemical-resistant Tekton coatings for power generation facilities — protecting boilers, cooling towers, flue systems, and structural assets from the aggressive thermal and chemical environments of continuous power generation.

Offshore Platform Corrosion Protection

Subsea and topsides corrosion protection systems for offshore oil and gas platforms — proven in deepwater environments where salt, pressure, biological fouling, and mechanical wear combine to destroy conventional coatings within years.

Refinery Process Coatings

Specialty protective coatings for refinery vessels, piping systems, and structural assets — resistant to the full range of hydrocarbon process chemicals, operating temperatures, and abrasive media encountered in petroleum refining.

Cryogenic Sealant Systems (LNG/H2)

Cryogenic-stable sealant formulations maintaining flexibility and bond strength at extreme low temperatures for LNG storage, hydrogen infrastructure, and aerospace fuel system applications where conventional sealants fail.

XContainers Technology

XContainers™ — Advanced Containment Systems

XContainers represent a breakthrough in energy storage and containment engineering — fusing Theis Tekton's self-healing polymer chemistry with purpose-built structural containment to eliminate the risk of catastrophic leak and spill events across energy, maritime, and industrial applications.

Self-Healing Fuel Tank Technology

XContainer fuel tanks incorporate Theis Tekton's self-healing polymer layer — autonomously sealing punctures and micro-fractures without intervention. Field-validated in maritime applications where hull puncture events would otherwise result in catastrophic fuel release and environmental liability. Tanks remain structurally sound and mission-capable after damage events that destroy conventional tank systems.

Energy Storage Containment

XContainers are engineered for the rigorous demands of stationary energy storage — protecting battery arrays, chemical energy media, and liquid fuel reserves in power generation, grid storage, and industrial facilities. Superior chemical resistance and barrier performance guard against electrolyte leakage, thermal event propagation, and the corrosive environments common to industrial energy infrastructure.

Case Study: Maritime Fuel Containment

XContainer technology was deployed aboard maritime vessels operating in contested waters where conventional fuel tank puncture would render the vessel non-operational. The self-healing Tekton liner maintained fuel containment integrity after ballistic and debris impact events — enabling continued vessel operation and eliminating catastrophic fuel spill risk in environmentally sensitive waterways.

Industrial Hazardous Fluid Containment

XContainers provide secondary containment for industrial chemical storage — dramatically reducing spill risk in refinery, pipeline, and power generation environments. The Tekton lining resists the full range of hydrocarbon, solvent, and chemical process media, providing a lifetime containment barrier that reduces insurance liability and environmental compliance cost.

Absorbtec Technology

Absorbtec™ — Filtration & Spill Control

Absorbtec filtration technology addresses the energy industry's most persistent environmental liability: the removal of hydrocarbons and difficult contaminants from water and process streams. Licensed and distributed through Theis's clean-technology portfolio, Absorbtec is a comprehensive system encompassing absorption, filtration, and spill containment from source to remediation.

◈

Hydrocarbon Filtration

Advanced filtration media removes oil, fuel, and hydrocarbon contamination from produced water, stormwater, and process effluent streams — meeting strict environmental discharge standards at energy production and processing facilities.

◈

Heavy Metals Removal

Absorbtec water filtration systems target the most chemically challenging contaminants — heavy metals including lead, mercury, arsenic, and cadmium — delivering treated water quality that exceeds EPA standards for industrial and potable applications.

◈

Spill Containment Systems

Integrated spill containment systems paired with Absorbtec filtration provide a complete response-to-remediation capability — from immediate spill absorption through secondary containment to final effluent polishing before discharge or reuse.

◈

Produced Water Treatment

Oil and gas produced water — among the highest-volume industrial waste streams in the US — contains complex hydrocarbon and dissolved solids loads that standard treatment cannot address. Absorbtec is engineered specifically for the treatment challenges of upstream produced water management.

◈

Environmental Compliance

Absorbtec systems provide the documented treatment performance and compliance chain-of-custody that energy operators require to satisfy state and federal environmental discharge permits — reducing regulatory exposure and environmental remediation liability.

◈

Pipeline & Tank Spill Response

Rapid-deployment Absorbtec spill response kits provide immediate hydrocarbon absorption for pipeline integrity events, tank overfill incidents, and transportation spills — containing contamination before it reaches soil, groundwater, or waterway resources.

Wind Power Energy

Theis Wind Power Energy (WPE)

Theis Wind Power Energy is in prototype development — a quantum leap in turbine technology approaching the Levelized Cost of Energy of coal while delivering a 98% reduction in CO₂ emissions versus coal and petroleum. WPE represents a long-term strategic technology initiative. It is not a current commercial offering — it is showcased here as an emerging technology in active development.
Advanced Turbine Design — Gyroscopically and harmonically stable architecture engineered for superior performance at lower altitudes and in extreme wind velocities.
Wind & Solar Combined — Single integrated unit harvests both wind and solar energy, maximizing power output per square foot of installation footprint.
Advanced Materials — Lighter and stronger than conventional turbine materials, optimized for lower altitude operation where traditional designs underperform.
Readily Available Components — Designed around commercially available parts to reduce supply chain risk and lower manufacturing cost at scale.
Minimal Avian Impact — Design philosophy and specialized blade geometry are engineered to minimize harm to native bird populations — a key regulatory differentiator.
Specialized Blade Coating — Proprietary blade surface treatment optimizes aerodynamic performance and extends blade service life in harsh environments.
Shipping Container Distribution — The full turbine system ships inside a standard intermodal container, dramatically simplifying global logistics versus conventional large-turbine transport.
Ease of Installation — Smaller components and shorter towers reduce installation complexity, crane requirements, and site preparation cost compared to utility-scale conventional turbines.
Reduced Maintenance — Gyroscopically stable design and advanced materials significantly reduce maintenance frequency and cost over the turbine's service life.
Offshore Superiority — Size, height profile, and MW/sq-ft output make WPE a superior candidate for offshore implementation where conventional turbine logistics and foundation requirements are prohibitive.
DOE Policy Alignment — WPE technology development is structured to align with Department of Energy wind energy policy frameworks and funding priorities.
Multi-Sector Power Vision — WPE power generation is designed to ultimately support vehicles, aircraft, rail, and maritime transportation — enabling cross-sector clean energy deployment.
