Theis Energy

Protecting the infrastructure that powers America.

Overview

Advanced Materials for Energy Infrastructure

Theis Energy applies Tekton-based coatings and petroleum engineering expertise to protect critical oil, gas, power, and energy infrastructure from corrosion, pressure failure, and environmental degradation. Our pipeline coating systems, tank protection products, and power plant engineering extend asset life and reduce catastrophic failure risk.

From patented underground storage tank systems to anti-corrosion pipeline coatings proven in offshore environments, Theis Energy delivers the materials science that keeps America's energy infrastructure running. Our solutions protect the assets that cannot fail — eliminating the environmental liability and economic loss that corrosion-driven failures create.

Products & Solutions

What We Offer

Pipeline Anti-Corrosion Coating Systems

Long-life Tekton-based coating systems for oil, gas, and water pipelines — providing chemical, abrasion, and microbial corrosion resistance far exceeding conventional epoxy and polyurethane pipeline coatings.

Underground Storage Tank Systems (Patented)

Patented form-coded underground storage tank fill port and lining systems — preventing cross-contamination, extending structural life, and eliminating the environmental liability of corroded underground fuel storage assets.

Power Plant Protective Coatings

High-temperature, chemical-resistant Tekton coatings for power generation facilities — protecting boilers, cooling towers, flue systems, and structural assets from the aggressive thermal and chemical environments of continuous power generation.

Offshore Platform Corrosion Protection

Subsea and topsides corrosion protection systems for offshore oil and gas platforms — proven in deepwater environments where salt, pressure, biological fouling, and mechanical wear combine to destroy conventional coatings within years.

Refinery Process Coatings

Specialty protective coatings for refinery vessels, piping systems, and structural assets — resistant to the full range of hydrocarbon process chemicals, operating temperatures, and abrasive media encountered in petroleum refining.

Cryogenic Sealant Systems (LNG/H2)

Cryogenic-stable sealant formulations maintaining flexibility and bond strength at extreme low temperatures for LNG storage, hydrogen infrastructure, and aerospace fuel system applications where conventional sealants fail.
