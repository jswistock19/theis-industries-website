Overview

Specialty Construction Hardened by Advanced Materials

Theis Construction delivers specialty construction and infrastructure hardening services powered by Theis Tekton™ chemistry. From blast-resistant facilities to Tekton-reinforced concrete structures, we build infrastructure that performs under extreme conditions. Our team brings structural engineering excellence to the most demanding build environments in the world.

Our construction capabilities combine traditional structural engineering excellence with proprietary Tekton enhancement — producing structures that resist blast, ballistics, corrosion, and environmental degradation beyond what conventional construction can achieve. We build for the long term — facilities designed to outlast the threats they face.

Products & Solutions

What We Offer

Blast-Resistant Facility Construction

Design and construction of facilities engineered to survive blast overpressure events — including command centers, ammunition storage, critical infrastructure facilities, and hardened communication nodes.

Tekton-Enhanced Structural Systems

Structural concrete, steel, and composite systems enhanced with Theis Tekton™ chemistry — delivering superior impact resistance, corrosion protection, and service life compared to conventional structural methods.

Hardened Command & Control Facilities

Specialized construction of command, control, communications, and intelligence facilities requiring multi-threat protection, electromagnetic shielding, and continuity-of-operations design standards.

Corrosion-Protected Infrastructure

Civil and structural infrastructure construction with integrated Tekton corrosion protection — eliminating the premature deterioration that costs governments and operators billions annually in maintenance and replacement.

Specialty Coating Application

Professional application of Theis Tekton™ protective coatings to new and existing structures, bridges, tunnels, and critical infrastructure assets — providing a permanent barrier against blast, ballistics, and environmental degradation.

Retrofit Structural Hardening

Engineering and construction services to harden existing facilities against blast, ballistic, and natural hazard threats — extending service life and upgrading protection levels without full demolition and rebuild.
