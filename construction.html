Specialty Construction Hardened by Advanced Materials

Theis Construction delivers specialty construction and infrastructure hardening services powered by Theis Tekton™ chemistry. From blast-resistant facilities to Tekton-reinforced concrete structures, we build infrastructure that performs under extreme conditions. Our team brings structural engineering excellence to the most demanding build environments in the world.

Our construction capabilities combine traditional structural engineering excellence with proprietary Tekton enhancement — producing structures that resist blast, ballistics, corrosion, and environmental degradation beyond what conventional construction can achieve. We build for the long term — facilities designed to outlast the threats they face.
