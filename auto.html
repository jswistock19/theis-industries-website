Overview

Automotive Protection and Tekton-Enhanced Vehicle Products

Theis Auto produces Tekton-enhanced automotive protection products including mud flaps, undercoating systems, and protective coatings for commercial, consumer, and specialty vehicles. Our flagship product line — Tekton-enhanced mud flaps and vehicle protective coatings — delivers unmatched durability against road debris, salt, chemical exposure, and mechanical impact.

Theis Auto products are engineered with the same materials science that protects military vehicles and deep-sea platforms — now available for commercial and consumer automotive applications. Every product brings the Tekton heritage of extreme-environment performance to the vehicles that keep America's fleets, farms, and families moving.

Products & Solutions

What We Offer

Tekton-Enhanced Mud Flaps

Our flagship automotive product — mud flaps engineered with Tekton polymer chemistry delivering superior abrasion resistance, flexibility across extreme temperature ranges, and long service life compared to standard rubber or plastic alternatives.

Vehicle Undercoating Systems

Spray-applied Tekton undercoating for vehicle chassis, frame rails, and undercarriage components — providing permanent protection against road salt, moisture, gravel impact, and the electrochemical corrosion that destroys conventional undercoating within a few seasons.

Fleet Protective Coating Programs

Turnkey fleet protection programs providing Tekton coating application for commercial vehicle fleets — extending vehicle service life, reducing corrosion-related downtime, and lowering total cost of ownership for operators running large vehicle inventories.

Custom Automotive Coatings

Engineered-to-specification Tekton coatings for specialized automotive applications including bed liners, running boards, fender flares, and custom vehicle components requiring maximum impact and abrasion resistance.

Commercial Vehicle Protection

Heavy-duty protection systems for Class 6–8 trucks, trailers, and commercial vehicles — Tekton coatings and components engineered for the high-mileage, high-abuse environments of professional commercial vehicle operation.

Off-Road & Heavy Equipment Coatings

Tekton coating systems for off-road vehicles, agricultural equipment, and construction machinery — protecting against the rock impacts, chemical exposure, and environmental extremes that shorten the service life of conventional protective coatings.
