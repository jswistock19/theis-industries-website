Overview

Automotive Protection and Tekton-Enhanced Vehicle Products

Theis Auto produces Tekton-enhanced automotive protection products including mud flaps, undercoating systems, and protective coatings for commercial, consumer, and specialty vehicles. Our flagship product line — Tekton-enhanced mud flaps and vehicle protective coatings — delivers unmatched durability against road debris, salt, chemical exposure, and mechanical impact.

Theis Auto products are engineered with the same materials science that protects military vehicles and deep-sea platforms — now available for commercial and consumer automotive applications. Every product brings the Tekton heritage of extreme-environment performance to the vehicles that keep America's fleets, farms, and families moving.

Waste Management — 5,000 Dumpster Bodies

Theis Industries coated 5,000 commercial dumpster bodies with Theis Tekton for Waste Management. Tekton fully sealed each dumpster body to prevent leaking and unsightly messes — including complete resistance to the highly corrosive nature of decomposing milk and organic waste streams.

Case Study — Motorsport Safety

NASCAR — 470 High-Impact Deflection Shields

Theis Industries manufactured 470 yellow, high-impact resistant deflection shields (front fascia) for the NASCAR safety program. Shields were required on every car in the race. Material testing was conducted by spinning samples in a centrifuge at approximately 40,000 RPM — then placing a 6-inch iron peg in the path of the spinning material.

Theis Tekton passed with flying colors: the material deformed on impact but remained intact and performed exactly as intended. The shields were delivered to the NASCAR safety organization and installed on every competing vehicle, with some shields painted to match car liveries while maintaining required safety performance. Designed to withstand crash impact forces approaching 440 mph.

Engineering Spotlight

NASCAR Deflection Shields — By the Numbers

When NASCAR required a high-impact deflection shield capable of surviving crash forces at professional racing speeds, Theis Industries was selected to manufacture the front fascia safety components for the entire field. The engineering challenge: produce a component that could deform to absorb energy while remaining structurally intact — protecting driver and surrounding vehicles at impact speeds approaching 440 mph.

Testing was extreme. Material samples were placed in a centrifuge system spinning at approximately 40,000 RPM — then a 6-inch iron peg was inserted into the path of the spinning Tekton material. The material was effectively acting as a propeller striking a stationary obstacle at high speed. Theis Tekton deformed on contact and remained intact — precisely the performance profile required for a safety shield.

All 470 shields were delivered to the NASCAR safety organization and mandated on every car in the race. Some shields were painted to match individual car liveries, with the Tekton substrate maintaining full safety performance regardless of the paint coating applied.
