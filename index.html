Defense Applications

Blast & Ballistics

We excel in all areas of advanced defensive technologies — lightweight, cost-effective, and battle-proven. Our solutions outperform current market standards.

Body Armor

+

Lightweight personal protection systems with multi-strike capability, little to no back face deformation, and superior ballistic defense over current market offerings. Incorporates Theis Tekton for unmatched flexibility and field durability.

Vehicle Armor

+

Moldable vehicle armor solutions that are impervious to moisture and salt water, offering significant weight savings without sacrificing protection levels. Compatible with legacy and next-gen platforms.

Structural Armor

+

Protect critical infrastructure, buildings, command centers, and facilities with structural armor systems designed for blast resistance and multi-hit ballistic threat mitigation.

Blast Protection

Specialty Coatings & Stealth

+

Questions

Frequently Asked

What is Theis Tekton / Liquid Kevlar? +
Theis Tekton is our proprietary materials system — a revolutionary compound that can be engineered to be as flexible as chewing gum or as rigid as steel, on demand. It self-heals, cures in seconds, and has been deployed in deep-sea, aerospace, defense, and industrial applications for over 40 years. It is sometimes referred to as Liquid Kevlar due to its extraordinary strength-to-weight and protective characteristics.
What industries do you serve? +
We serve defense, aerospace, marine, energy, industrial manufacturing, medical, infrastructure, oil & gas, and more. If extreme environments, advanced materials, or impossible engineering problems are involved — we're the right call.
How do we engage with Theis Industries? +
Start by contacting us with your challenge or interest. We offer technology sale, licensing, partnerships, contract manufacturing, R&D consulting, and lease arrangements. We tailor the engagement model to what makes sense for your program or business.
Are your technologies available for government and DoD contracts? +
Yes. Our defense technologies — including body armor, vehicle armor, structural blast protection, and specialty coatings — are engineered to meet the demands of government and Department of Defense programs. We welcome inquiries from prime contractors, OEMs, and government acquisition offices.
Can you develop a custom solution for a problem we haven't solved yet? +
That's exactly what we do best. We shine when the mission seems impossible. Our interdisciplinary team of engineers and scientists thrives on novel, complex, and high-stakes problems that others have walked away from.
Where are you based and do you work internationally? +
Theis Industries is U.S.-based. Our technologies have been deployed globally — from deep-sea installations to space programs — and we work with international partners, governments, and corporations.
