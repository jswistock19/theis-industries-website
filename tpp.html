Overview

Utility Infrastructure That Outlasts Everything Else

TPP produces Theis Tekton™-coated utility poles and pipe systems for electrical, telecommunications, and civil infrastructure. By applying the Tekton coating system to traditional poles and pipes, TPP dramatically extends service life, eliminates rot and corrosion, and reduces lifecycle costs for utilities and municipalities.

Traditional wood poles rot. Steel pipes corrode. Concrete cracks. TPP infrastructure — enhanced by Theis Tekton — does none of these. Our poles and pipe systems are engineered to perform for decades in saltwater, desert, arctic, and high-humidity environments where conventional materials fail within years of installation.

Products & Solutions

What We Offer

Tekton-Coated Utility Poles

Wood and composite utility poles treated with the full Theis Tekton™ coating system — delivering superior rot resistance, moisture barrier performance, and structural longevity far beyond untreated or pressure-treated alternatives.

Tekton-Lined Pipe Systems

Steel and composite pipe systems lined with Tekton polymer chemistry, providing complete corrosion protection for water, gas, and industrial fluid transport in aggressive underground and exposed environments.

Corrosion-Resistant Telecom Poles

Utility poles specifically configured for telecommunications and broadband infrastructure, engineered with Tekton chemistry to resist coastal salt spray, chemical exposure, and decades of environmental cycling.

Marine & Coastal Pole Systems

Specialized pole configurations for coastal, tidal, and marine environments — where salt, humidity, wave action, and biological fouling destroy conventional materials in a fraction of their expected service life.

Retrofit Pole Coating Service

On-site or factory application of Tekton coatings to existing utility poles and structures, extending service life without full replacement — reducing cost and infrastructure disruption for utilities and municipalities.

Custom Infrastructure Coatings

Engineered-to-specification coating solutions for non-standard infrastructure applications — including bridge supports, transmission towers, water treatment structures, and specialty civil infrastructure assets.

The railroad industry presents significant, underserved opportunities for Theis Industries across four distinct categories. Each represents a clear competitive advantage — backed by Tekton chemistry — in a market where incumbent solutions are expensive, inadequate, or both.

Fastener Restoration

SpikeFast — the incumbent fastener restoration product — sells for approximately $120/gallon. Its patent has expired.

Theis can manufacture the equivalent product for $60/gallon and bring it to market under a new brand at a significant discount — while maintaining strong margin.

02

Spill Containment

Railroads use hydrocarbons, solvents, caustics, acids, and heavy metals — all of which degrade costly spill containment systems and leach through into the ground.

Competitor PolyStar allows hydrocarbons to leach through fiberglass. Can't be repaired. Requires full replacement.

Theis Tekton system: $12/sq ft applied cost, sells for $25+/sq ft. Fully waterproof, return to service in hours.

Water Filtration

Heavy metals, hydrocarbons, and other contaminants are endemic in rail yards. Regulatory compliance requires removing the most difficult contaminants from water discharge.

Case Study

The #20 Locomotive

A real-world stress test no lab could replicate — and a result no conventional coating could survive.

The $20 Locomotive

Proven Under Extreme Real-World Conditions

A rail line engaged Theis Industries for multiple projects. During one engagement, the #20 Locomotive was coated with Theis Tekton on the front cow catcher and front nose of the locomotive — primarily to maintain a clean, professional appearance for longer than standard paint or coatings could deliver.

What happened next was unplanned, unscripted, and unambiguous.

The #20 Locomotive was hijacked. Driven off the tracks. Sent through concrete barriers and steel fencing. When the chaos settled and the dust cleared, the investigating team realized — the locomotive involved was the one previously coated with Theis Tekton for testing.

Upon inspection of the coated surfaces: absolutely no signs of damage. After being dusted off, the coating looked as good as the day it was applied.

"No Damage. Looked as good as new. Experienced way more damaging activity than usual and came out clean."

Result

Driven off rails. Through concrete barriers. Through steel fencing. Zero coating damage. The Theis Tekton system performed beyond any reasonable test parameter — and proved its value in the most definitive way possible.
