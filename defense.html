Overview

Next-Generation Defensive Technologies for the Modern Threat Environment

Theis Defense — operating under the Alviz brand — delivers the full spectrum of defensive technologies built on Theis Tekton™ chemistry. Body armor, vehicle armor, blast protection, structural defense, and specialty coatings — all engineered to outperform current market standards in weight, protection level, and field durability.

Where legacy armor is heavy, rigid, and single-use, Theis Defense solutions are lightweight, moldable, multi-strike, and self-healing. The Alviz line is engineered specifically for DoD, law enforcement, and allied government procurement — with ITAR awareness and full compliance posture for sensitive defense applications.

Products & Solutions

What We Offer

Lightweight Body Armor Systems

Tekton-based body armor achieving multi-strike capability at weight classes significantly below legacy ceramic and steel plate systems — engineered for warrior performance and sustained wear in operational environments.

Moldable Vehicle Armor

Tekton-based vehicle armor that conforms to complex vehicle geometries and legacy platform contours — providing ballistic and blast protection without the weight penalties of conventional steel or ceramic armor packages.

Spray-Applied Retrofit Armor

Field and depot-applied spray armor coating that bonds directly to existing vehicle surfaces — enabling rapid ballistic and blast upgrading of legacy platforms without structural modification or extended downtime.

Blast & IED Attenuation Panels

Tekton-engineered panels and structural inserts for vehicle floors, doors, and hull surfaces — attenuating IED blast overpressure and fragmentation penetration to protect crew compartment occupants.

Structural Ballistic Protection

Ballistic protection systems for fixed structures, command posts, guard positions, and critical facility walls — engineered for rapid installation, modular reconfiguration, and multi-hit rated performance.

Specialty Defense Coatings

Radar-absorbent, thermal barrier, and camouflage-compatible specialty coatings for aerospace, naval, and ground defense platforms — providing signature management alongside ballistic and corrosion protection.
