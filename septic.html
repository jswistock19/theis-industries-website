Overview

Tekton-Lined Septic Systems Built to Outlast Everything

Theis Septic applies Tekton polymer chemistry to septic systems, wastewater infrastructure, and underground utility installations — delivering corrosion resistance and structural integrity that standard materials cannot match. Traditional concrete septic systems crack, corrode, and fail. Steel systems rust. Theis Septic systems are lined with Tekton.

Our Tekton-lined tanks and wastewater structures are impervious to the chemical attack, moisture, and microbial corrosion that destroys conventional systems over time. The result is a septic and wastewater system that lasts decades longer with dramatically lower maintenance cost — a significant advantage for residential, commercial, and municipal customers who want to install once and forget it.

Products & Solutions

What We Offer

Tekton-Lined Septic Tanks

Precast and custom septic tanks with a full Tekton polymer interior lining — permanently bonded to the tank structure to prevent hydrogen sulfide attack, chemical corrosion, and microbial degradation that destroys conventional concrete tanks.

Polymer-Coated Leach Field Systems

Leach field distribution pipes and structures coated with Tekton chemistry — resisting root intrusion, soil chemistry attack, and the biological degradation that shortens the effective life of conventional leach field installations.

Corrosion-Resistant Wastewater Structures

Manholes, junction boxes, pump stations, and wastewater structures constructed or lined with Tekton chemistry — providing the long-term integrity that municipal and industrial wastewater systems require.

Commercial & Industrial Septic Systems

High-capacity septic and wastewater management systems for commercial facilities, restaurants, campgrounds, industrial plants, and institutional properties generating waste streams that rapidly degrade conventional systems.

Retrofit Tank Lining Service

On-site application of Tekton lining to existing septic tanks, wastewater structures, and underground utility chambers — restoring structural integrity and extending service life without full excavation and replacement.

Custom Underground Utility Coating

Engineered-to-specification Tekton coating for non-standard underground utility structures — including vaults, conduit systems, electrical duct banks, and specialty subsurface infrastructure requiring long-term corrosion protection.
