About Theis Industries

A technology and engineering company built on one core belief: if physics, chemistry, and engineering can solve a problem — we will solve it.

Our Story

Theis Industries Inc. was built by engineers and scientists who refused to accept "it can't be done" as an answer. We have spent over two decades developing proprietary technologies and deploying deep engineering expertise in the world's most demanding environments — from the crushing pressure of the ocean floor to the harsh vacuum of outer space.

Our flagship technology, Theis Tekton™ — also known as Liquid Kevlar — emerged from years of materials science research and field testing. It is now a proven platform with applications across defense, aerospace, marine, energy, and industrial markets.

We are a U.S.-based technology company. Our technologies are available for sale, license, partnership, and ongoing contract production. We work with defense contractors, government agencies, industrial companies, and innovators worldwide.
