HVAC / Thermal Engineering
Bikram Yoga — Radisson LAX
Extreme Heat and Clean Air for Bikram Yoga
Challenge

Bikram Yoga — practiced by 18,000+ teachers across 60 countries — requires rooms held at 105°F. Teacher trainings at Radisson LAX faced two critical problems: achieving sufficient heat and eliminating odor from large class sizes.

Solution

Jason engineered a proprietary blend of gases with a directed airflow system that kept heat concentrated near the floor rather than rising, while neutralizing odor-causing bacteria entirely. Doorknobs became too hot to touch — the room was at optimal temperature throughout.

"Jason made HEAVY HEAT and the room smelt beautiful even after all the classes! I never thought it was possible!"

— Bikram Choudhry, Founder of Bikram Yoga

Extreme Custom Engineering
Contessa Shrimp — Underground Tennis Court
Unique Circumstances Requiring Extreme Engineering
Challenge

City ordinances denied a permit for an outdoor tennis court due to unacceptable light and noise impact on the surrounding neighborhood. The court had to be built — but it could not be built above ground.

Solution

Theis engineered a full tennis court 80 feet below ground, carved into the hillside and finished in old-world plaster bath house aesthetics. The court connects to the main structure via an underground tunnel. World-famous old-world plasterer Dominic La Ferla was contracted for the interior finish.

Result

An immaculate piece of engineering — a fully functional, regulation-size tennis court invisible to the city, neighbors, and any aerial observation.
