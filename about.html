About Theis Industries

A technology and engineering company built on one core belief: if physics, chemistry, and engineering can solve a problem — we will solve it.

Our Story

Theis Industries Inc. was built by engineers and scientists who refused to accept "it can't be done" as an answer. We have spent over two decades developing proprietary technologies and deploying deep engineering expertise in the world's most demanding environments — from the crushing pressure of the ocean floor to the harsh vacuum of outer space.

Our flagship technology, Theis Tekton™ — also known as Liquid Kevlar — emerged from years of materials science research and field testing. It is now a proven platform with applications across defense, aerospace, marine, energy, and industrial markets.

We are a U.S.-based technology company. Our technologies are available for sale, license, partnership, and ongoing contract production. We work with defense contractors, government agencies, industrial companies, and innovators worldwide.

Founder & Inventor

Jason Theis

Chief Scientist · Inventor of Theis Tekton™

Jason Theis is the founder, lead inventor, and chief scientist behind Theis Industries Inc. His career spans over two decades of materials science, biometric authentication, and protective systems engineering — with multiple issued U.S. patents and pending applications spanning defense ballistics, advanced polymers, marine and aerospace coatings, and vehicle security.

The Theis Tekton™ platform — publicly known as Liquid Kevlar™ — is the culmination of years of in-house chemistry and field testing in environments most materials fail in: the deep ocean, orbital platforms, active combat, and extreme industrial conditions.

Our Trajectory

Two Decades of Engineering the Impossible

A condensed look at the milestones behind Theis Industries' proprietary materials, IP portfolio, and field-proven deployments.

2003

Materials R&D Begins

Initial chemistry and polymer research that would become Theis Tekton™ — a variable-hardness, rapid-cure protective compound.

2006

First Patent Filings

Biometric anti-theft and operator-verification systems filed and prosecuted — establishing the company's IP foundation.

2007 – 2014

Patents Issued

Multiple issued U.S. patents covering biometric device security, operator verification, and authentication systems.

2010 – 2020

Tekton Field-Proven

Theis Tekton™ deployed and validated in deep-sea, aerospace, and defense environments — establishing real-world performance no laboratory can replicate.

2024

Industrial Energy Patent

Form-coded underground fuel storage tank filling port system — cross-contamination prevention for energy infrastructure.

2025+

Commercialization Acceleration
